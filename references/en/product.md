# Product, Communication and Execution

English companion to [the Chinese guide](../product.md), which contains source links.

## 5W1H / 5W2H
- **Use:** Clarify incomplete information about a task, event or plan.
- **Do:** Ask What, Why, Who, When, Where and How; add How much/How many where cost or quantity matters.
- **Limits:** This information checklist does not prove causation and differs from the repeated causal inquiry called Five Whys. Mark unknowns.

## RICE prioritization
- **Use:** Compare product initiatives competing for resources.
- **Do:** For a consistent period estimate Reach, Impact, Confidence and Effort; compare R × I × C ÷ E, documenting estimates, dependencies and hard constraints.
- **Limits:** Scores contain subjective judgments and cannot override safety, compliance or prerequisites.

## Objectives and Key Results (OKRs)
- **Use:** Translate a team's direction into measurable outcomes.
- **Do:** Define a directional objective and a few measurable, time-bound results with baselines, owners and review points.
- **Limits:** Activities are not automatically outcomes; numerical targets do not replace quality, safety or actual authority.

## Value proposition canvas
- **Use:** Check whether an offer addresses the needs of a specific user segment.
- **Do:** Map customer jobs, pains and gains to how the offer helps; validate with actual interviews and behavioral evidence.
- **Limits:** A filled canvas is a hypothesis, not proof of product-market fit; segments differ.

## Business model canvas
- **Use:** Describe how a business creates, delivers and captures value.
- **Do:** Map customers, value proposition, channels, relationships, revenue, resources, activities, partners and costs; identify dependencies and risky assumptions.
- **Limits:** A completed canvas does not establish profitability or viability; update as evidence changes.

## Opportunity solution tree
- **Use:** Identify which user problems to address before selecting solutions.
- **Do:** Define the outcome; use research to identify opportunities, generate alternative solutions and distinguishing experiments, and revise the tree.
- **Limits:** Do not disguise predetermined features as opportunities; interviews and tests must actually be conducted.

## Working backwards: PR/FAQ
- **Use:** Clarify customer value and feasibility before building a product or initiating change.
- **Do:** Draft a future-oriented customer outcome and FAQs on value, feasibility, costs, risks and assumptions; plan evidence collection.
- **Limits:** Future claims are hypotheses, not events; never present invented figures or testimonials as real.

## A3 problem solving
- **Use:** Communicate a problem, evidence, countermeasures and follow-up across teams.
- **Do:** Summarize context, current condition, target, cause hypotheses, action and checks; update through investigation with relevant participants.
- **Limits:** A3 is a thinking process, not just filling one page; label unverified causes.
